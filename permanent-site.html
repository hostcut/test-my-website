The quiet tax

Most people don’t need a website platform. They need a website.

You already decided a site makes sense. The annoyance is what comes after: monthly platform fees, builder subscriptions, and hosting charges that never stop — just to keep the same pages online.

Over a few years that “cheap” $20–30/month turns into real money. And you still don’t own the thing. You’re renting a seat on someone else’s system.

  • Monthly or annual fees just so the site stays live
  • Your content locked inside a proprietary builder
  • Pricing and features that can change under you
  • Export options that feel incomplete or awkward
  • A constant low-grade feeling that you’re paying rent on something that should be yours
