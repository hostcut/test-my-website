How the full move works

Four clear steps. You keep ownership the entire time.

The goal is a clean hand-off so you never depend on us for day-to-day operation of the site.

  1. 01

    Review & scope

    We look at your current site, forms, and any special requirements. You get a fixed quote for the redesign or careful port.

  2. 02

    Rebuild on GitHub

    We create a private repository under your GitHub account (or transfer it to you) and build a modern, fast version of the site.

  3. 03

    Deploy to Cloudflare Pages

    The site goes live on Cloudflare Pages. We connect your domain, enable the free security layer, and apply the full rule set.

  4. 04

    You confirm everything works. Then you can cancel the old Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, or shared-hosting subscription. You keep both accounts.
